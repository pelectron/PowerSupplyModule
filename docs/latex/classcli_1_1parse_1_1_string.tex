A parser for strings. 

Strings in this context are a continous sequence of visible/printable characters, that is characters in the range 0x21 to 0x7E inclusive.

Strings may be enclosed in "{} quotes.  In that case, 0x20 (space) can also be in the string content. The character "{} can be escaped with backslash.
